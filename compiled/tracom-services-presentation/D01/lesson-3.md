# The live demo

If you have a Cardano wallet with you, follow along on your device. If not, watch the screen. You will see exactly what a Tracom Academy student sees the first time they log in.

Step 1: Connect your wallet. The platform supports Eternl, Nami, and other Cardano wallets. No email. No password. Your wallet is your identity.

Step 2: Mint your access token. To enroll in any course, a student mints a one-time access token from the platform. It is an on-chain transaction and the student's commitment to the programme.

Step 3: Enroll and explore. Once the access token confirms on-chain, the student can browse courses, enroll, and start working through lessons and assignments.

At the end of this demo, anyone who followed along with a wallet can claim an attendance credential: a real Cardano native asset that records you were here.

That credential will still be in your wallet ten years from now.
