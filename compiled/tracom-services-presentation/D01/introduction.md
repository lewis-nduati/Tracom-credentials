# Welcome to the demo

You are not watching a presentation. You are inside the product.

What you are about to experience is what a Tracom Academy student goes through when they enroll in a course. The only difference: at the end, your credential will say you were here for this.

This module has four parts. By the final one, anyone with a Cardano wallet in the room will have an on-chain credential in their wallet.

If you do not have a wallet, watch the screen. You will see exactly what the student sees.
