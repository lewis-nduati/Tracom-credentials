# The problem with paper credentials

Students across Kenya complete training programmes, pass assessments, and walk away with a certificate printed on paper.

That paper fails them in three ways.

A certificate is only as trustworthy as the institution that issued it. Anyone with a printer and a logo can produce something that looks legitimate. Employers know this, which is why many do not trust certificates they cannot verify independently.

Verifying one means calling the training provider. If the provider is busy, closed, or no longer exists, verification is impossible. Most employers do not bother. The certificate ends up in a drawer.

The student does not own the record either. The training provider holds the data. If they change their system, lose their files, or shut down, the student's evidence of learning goes with them.

That is what Tracom Academy is building against.
