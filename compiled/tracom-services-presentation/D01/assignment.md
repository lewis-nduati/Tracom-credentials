# Claim your attendance credential

If you have a Cardano wallet, you can claim your attendance credential now: a native asset that records you were here, permanently on-chain.

Connect your wallet, go to your credentials page, and submit the claim transaction. Your credential will appear once the transaction confirms.

If you do not have a wallet, the Tracom Academy pioneer programme will open soon. You can enroll, complete real coursework, and earn your first credential.
