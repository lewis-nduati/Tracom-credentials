# On-chain credentials on Cardano

A credential on Cardano is not a PDF. It is not a record in a database someone else controls. It is a native asset that lives in the student's own wallet.

Once minted, it exists on the Cardano blockchain. No one can alter it or delete it. It does not depend on Tracom Academy's servers staying online.

The blockchain is public. Any employer can look up the credential by wallet address and confirm: this person completed this course, at this institution. That takes thirty seconds and no phone call.

The token lives in the student's wallet. Tracom Academy cannot take it back. It follows the student across jobs and across countries.

Cardano was built for this kind of use case: identity and financial inclusion in emerging markets. The Andamio protocol, which powers this platform, was designed to connect on-chain credentials to real learning outcomes.

This is what Tracom Academy is building.
