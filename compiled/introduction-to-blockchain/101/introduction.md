# What Is Blockchain?

 What makes a blockchain different from every database that came before it?

  A traditional database has an owner — a bank, a company, a government. They control what gets written, what gets changed, and what gets deleted.

   You trust the institution because you have no choice.

  A blockchain removes that requirement. The record is maintained by thousands of independent computers. No single entity controls it. No one can

  quietly edit the history.

  This module covers how that works — the structure of blocks and chains, the properties that make the system tamper-evident, and why these

  properties matter specifically for credentials and payments in Kenya.

  By the end, you will be able to explain blockchain in plain language to anyone — including someone who has never heard the word before.
