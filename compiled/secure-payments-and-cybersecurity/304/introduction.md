# Compliance and Incident Response

The controls in Modules 301 through 303 are not just good practice — many of them are legal requirements. Payment systems in Kenya operate under two overlapping compliance frameworks: PCI DSS, the international card industry standard, and the regulatory requirements of the Central Bank of Kenya.

Understanding these frameworks does not mean memorising every requirement. It means knowing which ones apply to the system you are building, and knowing what you are obligated to do when something goes wrong.

This module covers what a junior POS developer must know about compliance, and what the first hour after a suspected breach looks like. Knowing the incident response steps before an incident happens is the difference between a contained event and a reportable breach.
