# Security Checklist and Incident Response Plan

This assignment produces two documents that become part of your capstone project submission.

**Part 1 — Security checklist**

Produce a checklist of the security controls your POS integration will implement. The checklist must cover at least one item from each of the four modules in this course:

- One or more items from the attack surface (Module 301)
- One or more items from encryption and tokenization (Module 302)
- One or more items from authentication and access control (Module 303)
- One or more items from compliance (Module 304)

For each item on the checklist, mark it as: Implemented / Planned / Not applicable (with reason).

**Part 2 — Incident response plan**

Write a one-page incident response plan for your POS integration. It must include:

1. Three specific indicators that would make you suspect a breach
2. The five response steps from Module 304, adapted to your specific integration (with real contact points where you have them)
3. One sentence on each: what evidence you would preserve, and what your client's CBK reporting obligation is

What to submit: Your security checklist and your incident response plan.

You will pass this assignment if you:

- Cover all four course modules in the checklist with at least one concrete, specific control each
- Adapt the incident response steps to your integration rather than restating them generically
- Correctly describe the CBK reporting obligation as one that applies to the licensed entity your client works with, not to you as a developer
