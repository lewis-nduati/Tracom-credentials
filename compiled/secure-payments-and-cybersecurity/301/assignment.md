# Map the Attack Surface of Your POS Integration

Take the architecture diagram you produced in Module 204. For each component and each connection in that diagram, identify the attack vectors it is exposed to.

Produce a table with these columns:

| Component / Connection | Attack vector | What the attacker gains | Severity (High / Medium / Low) |

You must include at least one entry for each of the four attack types covered in this module: skimming, man-in-the-middle, injection, and insider threat.

Then answer this question in 2–3 sentences: Which single point in your architecture would cause the most damage if compromised, and why?

What to submit: Your attack surface table and your answer to the highest-impact question.

You will pass this assignment if you:

- Correctly map each of the four attack types to at least one specific component or connection in your own architecture
- Assign severity ratings that are consistent with the potential financial or data impact
- Identify the highest-impact point with a technically sound justification
