# Payment Gateways and the Money Rail

When a transaction is approved in under two seconds, it feels instant. But money does not actually move in that moment. What moves is a message — a promise — that travels through a chain of banks and networks, each one passing the request to the next.

Understanding this chain is what separates developers who can build integrations from developers who can debug them. When something goes wrong — and it will — you need to know where in the chain the failure happened in order to fix it.

This module explains how the payment network works, why mobile money in East Africa operates differently from card payments, and what the difference between "approved" and "settled" actually means for the money in your merchant account.
