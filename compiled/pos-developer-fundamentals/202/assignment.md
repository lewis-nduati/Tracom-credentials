# Card vs M-Pesa: Transaction Walkthrough

Choose a real scenario: a customer buying groceries at a Nairobi supermarket that accepts both card and M-Pesa.

Write two separate transaction walkthroughs — one using a Visa card, one using M-Pesa — covering every step from the customer's action to the money arriving in the merchant's account. For each step, identify which system is responsible.

Then answer this question in 2–3 sentences: A card transaction was authorized at 3pm on Friday. The merchant calls you on Saturday asking why the money is not in their account yet. What do you tell them?

What to submit: Your two walkthroughs and your answer to the settlement question.

You will pass this assignment if you:

- Correctly show the pull-based nature of card and the push/callback nature of M-Pesa
- Include the settlement stage in the card walkthrough (not just authorization)
- Give a technically correct explanation of why authorized funds are not yet in the merchant's account
